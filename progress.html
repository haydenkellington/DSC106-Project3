A Landscape Before and After Natural Disasters

Six starter visualizations using NASA MODIS Terra satellite imagery. Select an event to update every graphic.


NASA event information

Unless labeled otherwise, images use false-color MODIS bands 7-2-1. Clouds and smoke can obscure the ground; visible differences do not measure damage or establish cause. Later images may show events still in progress.

1. Earlier and later images

Two images of the same area, side by side.

2. Swipe comparison

Drag the divider or use the slider to compare the two dates.

3. Opacity comparison

Move the slider to fade between the earlier and later image. Blending may also combine clouds and smoke from different days.

4. Timeline

Choose a date or play the sequence. Dates are selected observations and are not equally spaced.

—

5. Natural color and false color

Compare two band combinations for the same date. Colors in the panels represent different combinations of light.

6. Linked zoom

Zoom or drag either image; both move together. Zooming enlarges the images without adding detail.

Source: NASA MODIS Terra imagery from NASA GIBS. Images for each event use the same geographic extent. See README.md for dates, locations, and source details.
